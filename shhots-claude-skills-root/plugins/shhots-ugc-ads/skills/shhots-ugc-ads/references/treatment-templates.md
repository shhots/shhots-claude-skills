# Treatment Templates

The treatment is the `treatment` parameter of `shhots_create_product_video`. It must cover: setting, camera, lighting, actions, pacing, product moments, CTA. Use only visible or user-supplied product facts. One template per Shhots video format below; replace bracketed slots.

## ugc
Setting: [everyday location the audience recognizes: bathroom counter, car, kitchen, desk]. Camera: handheld selfie framing, chest-up, slight movement for authenticity. Lighting: natural window light or soft indoor light, no studio polish. Actions: creator speaks to camera, picks up product at [7s], uses or demonstrates it naturally, reacts genuinely. Pacing: quick cuts every 2-3 seconds matching speech beats. Product moments: label readable at [7-10s], product in hand at CTA. CTA: creator holds product to camera and delivers the CTA line.

## cinematic
Setting: [minimal set matching brand palette]. Camera: slow dolly or orbit moves, shallow depth of field, macro inserts on product details. Lighting: dramatic key light with soft fill, controlled highlights on product surfaces. Actions: product-led, no dialogue; motion reveals texture and form. Pacing: unhurried, 3-4 second shots, one accelerating beat before the close. Product moments: hero reveal in the final third, label crisp. CTA: end card with product centered and CTA text.

## animated
Setting: stylized graphic environment in brand colors. Camera: simulated pans and zooms between motion-design scenes. Lighting: flat, high-contrast graphic lighting. Actions: the product anchors each scene while animated elements illustrate [use case or mechanism]. Pacing: energetic, beat-synced transitions. Product moments: product rendered accurately in every scene. CTA: animated end card.

## product_speaking
Setting: clean tabletop or contextual scene where the product "performs". Camera: locked-off medium shot with punch-ins on emphasis lines. Lighting: bright, even, friendly. Actions: the product delivers the script with subtle animated personality. Pacing: comedic timing, pauses on punchlines. Product moments: continuous. CTA: the product delivers the CTA directly.

## storefront
Setting: retail shelf or branded display context. Camera: smooth lateral glide along the display, settling on the product. Lighting: bright retail lighting, no harsh shadows. Actions: product among its context, then isolated as the focus. Pacing: steady, confident. Product moments: pack front legible in the settle shot. CTA: price-free CTA text over the closing frame unless the user supplied pricing.

## lifestyle
Setting: [real usage environment: morning kitchen, gym bag, evening couch, trailhead]. Camera: observational, natural angles, some over-shoulder shots. Lighting: true-to-time-of-day natural light. Actions: a person uses the product inside a routine, unforced. Pacing: relaxed, 2-4 second shots. Product moments: integrated into the action, one clear beauty beat. CTA: soft voiceover or end-card CTA.

## unboxing_asmr
Setting: clean tabletop, top-down and 45-degree angles. Camera: macro close-ups on hands, textures, seals, and surfaces. Lighting: soft directional light that catches material texture. Actions: deliberate unboxing: peel, lift, unwrap, place; ambient sound carries the video. Pacing: slow, tactile, no rush. Product moments: every reveal is a product moment; final composed flat-lay. CTA: minimal end-card text; sound stays primary.

## Script-to-treatment note for non-UGC formats
For cinematic, storefront, lifestyle, and unboxing_asmr the script is voiceover or on-screen text, not creator speech. Keep it shorter than the UGC word budget: the visuals carry more of the message.
