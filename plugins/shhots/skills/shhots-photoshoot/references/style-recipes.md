# Style Recipes by Category

Recipes are starting points for the `creativeDirection` paragraph. Adapt the bracketed slots to the actual product; keep one idea per image. Styles: **studio** (clean, controlled, product-first), **lifestyle** (in-context, human-adjacent), **editorial** (magazine-grade concept), **bold** (graphic, high-contrast, scroll-stopping).

## Skincare / Beauty
- **Studio:** Product centered on a [wet slate / matte ceramic] pedestal, smooth [neutral tone] sweep background, soft key light from upper left catching the label, gentle specular highlight along the container edge, product fills 60% of frame. Mood: clinical, calm, premium.
- **Lifestyle:** Product on a bathroom counter beside [towel, small plant], morning window light from the right, shallow depth of field, hand entering frame mid-reach, product in the front third. Mood: routine, fresh.
- **Editorial:** Product half-submerged in [water / cream texture] with slow ripples, top-down composition, single hard light creating defined shadow, negative space on the left. Mood: sculptural, luxurious.
- **Bold:** Product against a saturated [complementary color] backdrop, hard direct flash look, sharp shadow cast at 45 degrees, product slightly off-center, oversized in frame. Mood: loud, confident.

## Food & Beverage
- **Studio:** Pack front-facing on a [wood / stone] surface, plain sweep background a shade darker than the pack's dominant color, soft overhead light, crumbs or a single ingredient element at the base, label fully legible. Mood: appetizing, honest.
- **Lifestyle:** Product on a set table mid-meal, natural side light, hands and partial plates around it, steam or condensation if true to the product, product sharp against a softly blurred scene. Mood: shared, warm.
- **Editorial:** Ingredients suspended mid-air around the pack on a plain backdrop, frozen-motion feel, directional light from behind-left, product anchored center. Mood: dynamic, crafted.
- **Bold:** Pack repeated in a tight grid or flanked by oversized ingredient cutouts, flat graphic background in brand color, even high-key lighting. Mood: playful, punchy.

## Pet
- **Studio:** Product upright on a [soft textile / clean floor] with a leash or toy as a single prop, warm neutral backdrop, soft frontal light, label legible, product fills half the frame. Mood: trustworthy, warm.
- **Lifestyle:** Product beside a dog/cat in a [living room / trailhead / park] scene, golden-hour light, animal engaged with the product naturally, product in sharp focus. Mood: joyful, real.
- **Editorial:** Product on a pedestal with paw prints leading to it across a smooth studio floor, single spotlight, deep background falloff. Mood: witty, premium.
- **Bold:** Product oversized against a flat playful color, graphic paw motifs as shadow shapes, hard light. Mood: fun, energetic.

## Apparel / Accessories
- **Studio:** Item on an invisible form or clean flat-lay on [linen / paper] surface, even soft light, true-to-color rendering, texture visible in a raking highlight. Mood: precise, tactile.
- **Lifestyle:** Item worn/carried in a [street / cafe / commute] moment, natural light, candid framing, item in the sharpest plane. Mood: effortless, aspirational.
- **Editorial:** Item styled with strong shadow play against architectural background, low sun angle, generous negative space. Mood: fashion-forward.
- **Bold:** Item floating against saturated backdrop with hard rim light, exaggerated scale, diagonal composition. Mood: statement.

## Home & Living
- **Studio:** Product on a material that matches its use ([oak shelf / marble counter]), tone-on-tone backdrop, soft window-style light from one side, subtle shadow grounding it. Mood: calm, quality.
- **Lifestyle:** Product in a lived-in room scene at [time of day], one human trace (mug, open book), ambient natural light, product in the front plane. Mood: homely, real.
- **Editorial:** Product isolated in an unexpectedly grand or minimal architectural space, single light source, long shadows. Mood: gallery, considered.
- **Bold:** Product against color-blocked walls, hard geometric shadows, centered symmetry. Mood: graphic, modern.

## Electronics
- **Studio:** Device at a three-quarter angle on [dark brushed metal / matte white], controlled edge lighting tracing the silhouette, screen off or showing only what the user supplied, reflections managed. Mood: precise, premium.
- **Lifestyle:** Device in use on a [desk / kitchen counter / gym] scene, hands interacting, natural plus practical light sources, device sharp. Mood: capable, everyday.
- **Editorial:** Device levitating over its surface with a soft ground shadow, gradient background, one dramatic highlight. Mood: futuristic, clean.
- **Bold:** Device against neon-leaning gradient, hard specular highlights, tight crop on a distinctive detail. Mood: hype, sharp.

## Other / Unknown category
Ask what the product most resembles from the categories above and adapt that recipe set. When truly novel, default to: Studio = clean pedestal + soft key light + 60% frame fill; Lifestyle = the product's most common real usage moment with natural light.
