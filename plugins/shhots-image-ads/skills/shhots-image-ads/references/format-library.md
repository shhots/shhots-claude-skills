# Format Library

14 static ad formats. Each entry: what it is, funnel stage, the creativeDirection template for `shhots_create_product_image`, and notes. Replace bracketed slots; put all overlay text verbatim in quotes. Suggested Shhots imageStyle per format is a default, not a law.

## 1. Problem-solution
Cold traffic's safest format: name the pain, show the answer.
**Direction:** Split composition: upper third carries the headline "[pain in the audience's words]?", lower two-thirds shows the product in [context that answers it], soft directional light on the product, muted background so the headline pops, CTA button-style text "[CTA]" bottom right. Style: lifestyle or studio. Mood: relieved, direct.

## 2. Before/after
Visual contrast sells transformation.
**Direction:** Frame split vertically down the middle: left side "[before state]" scene in flat desaturated light labeled "Before", right side the same scene improved with the product present, warm balanced light, labeled "After"; product sharp on the right, headline across the top "[allowed transformation claim]". Style: lifestyle. Mood: honest contrast.
**Note:** only transformations the allowed claims support.

## 3. Testimonial card
A real customer's words as the hero.
**Direction:** Clean card layout on a [brand color] background: large quotation "[user-supplied real quote]" in the upper two-thirds, attribution "[first name, descriptor]", product photographed small and accurate in the lower corner, soft even light. Style: studio. Mood: trustworthy, calm.
**Note:** quote must be real and user-supplied with permission. No invented names or ratings.

## 4. Us-vs-the-old-way comparison
Two columns, one obvious winner.
**Direction:** Two-column table composition: left column headed "The old way" with 3 short pain lines "[...]", right column headed "[product name]" with 3 short allowed-claim lines "[...]", right column visually brighter with the product image at its base, checkmark motifs on the right, x motifs on the left. Style: bold. Mood: decisive.
**Note:** naming a real competitor requires the user to supply and own the comparison.

## 5. Benefit stack
Retargeting's workhorse: everything they get, at a glance.
**Direction:** Product centered at [40%] of frame on a clean [surface], 3-4 short benefit lines stacked beside it, each from the allowed list "[...]", thin connector lines from each benefit to the relevant product part, even studio light, headline "[core promise]". Style: studio. Mood: complete, confident.

## 6. Offer stack
The deal, itemized.
**Direction:** Product hero centered with soft spotlight, stacked offer lines "[item 1]", "[item 2]", "[bonus]" with a struck-through "[user-supplied compare-at price]" and the "[user-supplied price]" prominent, deadline line "[user-supplied deadline]" if any, high-contrast CTA "[CTA]". Style: bold. Mood: urgent but clean.
**Note:** prices and deadlines only from the user.

## 7. Founder POV
A note from the person who built it.
**Direction:** Casual, credible scene: founder's workspace or production space with the product in the foreground, handwritten-style headline "Why I made this", 2-3 short lines of founder copy "[user's own words]", natural window light, imperfect and human framing. Style: lifestyle. Mood: sincere.

## 8. Advertorial style
Reads like an article clipping, converts like an ad.
**Direction:** Editorial layout: serif headline "[curiosity-driven, claim-safe headline]", subhead line, the product photographed documentary-style mid-use as the "story image", caption line beneath it, generous white space, small "Sponsored" tag. Style: editorial. Mood: journalistic.
**Note:** never imitate a specific publication's masthead or trade dress.

## 9. Listicle
"3 reasons" scans faster than a paragraph.
**Direction:** Numbered layout: headline "[N] reasons [audience] switch to [product]", numbered lines 1-[N] each carrying one allowed claim "[...]", product angled at the base spanning the width, bright even light, one accent color for the numbers. Style: bold or studio. Mood: snackable.

## 10. Metric-first
One big number does the arguing. SaaS and app native, works for D2C with a real stat.
**Direction:** A single oversized figure "[user-supplied metric]" filling the upper half, one-line context beneath it "[what the metric means]", product or UI screenshot anchoring the lower third, restrained palette, strong typographic hierarchy. Style: studio. Mood: authoritative.
**Note:** the metric must be user-supplied and real.

## 11. Screenshot hero (SaaS/app)
The claim on top, the proof below, and they must match.
**Direction:** Upper third: bold claim "[one specific outcome claim]". Lower two-thirds: the user's UI screenshot set in a subtle device or browser frame, slightly tilted, the exact feature that delivers the claim visible and legible, soft shadow grounding it, CTA "[Start free trial / user CTA]". Style: studio. Mood: capable, modern.
**Note:** the headline must describe what the visible UI shows; a mismatch reads as a template.

## 12. Feature callout
The annotated product diagram.
**Direction:** Product large and centered at a three-quarter angle, 3-4 thin callout lines pointing to specific parts, each label a short allowed claim "[...]", clean [neutral] backdrop, precise even light so every labeled detail is legible. Style: studio. Mood: engineered, transparent.

## 13. Social proof wall
Volume of love, at a glance. Warm audiences.
**Direction:** Grid of 4-6 short review-card snippets, each "[user-supplied real quote, few words]" with first-name attribution, product photographed centrally overlapping the grid, warm light. Style: studio or bold. Mood: loved.
**Note:** every quote real and supplied. No star-rating graphics or third-party badges; recommend official badge assets in post if the user holds them.

## 14. Product hero
The classic: when the product is the argument.
**Direction:** Product at [60-70%] of frame on [material surface], one dramatic key light shaping it, deep or clean background per brand, one short headline "[brand promise from allowed claims]" and nothing else, CTA small and confident. Style: studio, editorial, or bold. Mood: iconic.

## Ratio guidance across all formats
4:3 or 1:1 for feed, 9:16 for Stories/Reels placements, 16:9 for display and landing pages, 3:4 for editorial crops. Text-heavy formats (4, 6, 9, 13) hold up best at 1:1 and 4:3; rebuild rather than crop them for 9:16.
